Introduction & Overview

Scope, institutional purpose, and foundational privacy principles

Welcome to HealthCity Chat (referred to herein as "HealthChat", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), owned and operated by Health City. This policy applies to our iOS mobile application and our designated endpoint services hosted at https://doe8.rknewyorkwebdesign.com.

HealthCity Chat is an enterprise-grade, closed communication and messaging platform created exclusively for healthcare directors, hospital executives, clinical department heads, physicians, and affiliated medical teams. Its primary function is to facilitate rapid, secure internal communications, clinical triage discussions, and administrative notifications within verified healthcare organizations.

Strict Commitment Against Commercial Data Exploitation:
HealthCity Chat is an internal institutional workflow system. We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, trade, or lease personal user information, clinical messages, or contact details to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers under any circumstances.

We operate in accordance with HIPAA-aware security principles (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act), administrative safeguards, and the stringent developer guidelines established by the Apple App Store Review Guidelines (specifically Guideline 5.1.1 - Data Collection and Storage). We adhere strictly to data minimization: we only collect the minimum data necessary to establish authenticated sessions and transmit real-time communications.

02

Information We Collect

Categorized breakdown of collected data types and their explicit purpose

In compliance with Apple Guideline 5.1.1(i), we clearly state what data is collected, how it is collected, and all uses of that data. The information collected is restricted to four distinct categories:

Account Information

What We Collect: Full legal or professional name, institutional email address (e.g., doctor@hospital.org), designated healthcare role (Director, Executive, Physician, Nurse Specialist), and profile picture/avatar uploaded by the user.

Purpose: To establish your organizational user profile, verify enterprise authorization, facilitate member discovery in internal directories, and display identity in clinical chat channels.

Messages & Media

What We Collect: Encrypted text messages, group channel discussions, direct communications, and media attachments (user-selected photos, clinical diagrams, document attachments).

Purpose: Strictly to deliver, synchronize, and store organizational communications among authorized colleagues across connected mobile and desktop sessions.

Device & Technical Diagnostics

What We Collect: Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device tokens, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens, iOS operating system version, device model, IP address, and application build number.

Purpose: Solely for push notification delivery, maintaining active WebSocket connections, rate limiting, intrusion detection, and mandatory security audit logging.

Biometric Authentication

What We Collect: Zero Biometric Data. We do NOT collect, record, read, or transmit fingerprint or facial geometry data.

Implementation: Apple Face ID and Touch ID authentication are processed exclusively on your iPhone by the Apple Secure Enclave via LocalAuthentication APIs. Our application receives only a binary success/failure token.

Explicit Biometric Privacy Clause (Apple Review Guideline 5.1.1):
HealthCity Chat utilizes iOS LocalAuthentication solely for instantaneous app unlocking when reopening the app. Biometric templates (Face ID and Touch ID mathematical models) are never collected, never accessible by our application, never stored in our databases, and never transmitted over any network.

Data Security, Encryption & Storage

Defense-in-depth architecture protecting healthcare communications

As a platform serving healthcare leaders and clinical personnel, data security is foundational to everything we build. We implement defense-in-depth technical, physical, and administrative safeguards:

  • End-to-End Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 & WSS): All communication between the iOS mobile application and our backend endpoints is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.3) and secure encrypted WebSockets (WSS). Cleartext HTTP transmissions are strictly prohibited.
  • Cryptographic Storage at Rest: Databases and file storage repositories containing uploaded avatars, images, and chat transcripts are protected with industry-standard AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Least Privilege & Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC): Administrative access to underlying database clusters is restricted to authorized systems personnel via multi-factor authentication (MFA) and hardware security keys.
  • Immutable Audit Logging: Critical events—such as authentication requests, password updates, group invitation issuance, and administrative data modifications—are recorded in tamper-evident security audit trails to protect against unauthorized access.
  • Data Retention Periods: Active messages and media are retained as long as your organization maintains an active subscription or until deleted by the user or organization administrator.

Account Deletion & User Rights

Full compliance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v) for complete data erasure

In strict compliance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), all users of HealthCity Chat have the unequivocal right to initiate the complete and permanent deletion of their account, personal identifiers, sent messages, and uploaded content.

We believe you should have complete ownership over your digital footprint. As an authorized healthcare professional or director, you enjoy the following rights:

Right to Access & Portability

Request an export of the personal account information, message records, and media you have submitted to HealthCity Chat.

Right to Rectification

Update or correct inaccurate biographical details, change your profile photograph, or adjust departmental title info.

Right to Erasure (Permanent Deletion)

Request the complete, irreversible purging of your profile, authentication records, device tokens, and personal data from our servers.

Step-by-Step Instructions to Delete Your Account

To fulfill Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v), we provide clear, accessible, and direct methods for account deletion:

1

Option A: In-App Self-Service Deletion

Open HealthCity Chat on your iOS device > Navigate to Settings / Profile > Tap Account Security > Select "Delete My Account" > Confirm deletion.

2

Option B: Direct Email Request

Submit an explicit request from your registered work email to fahimmahtab5@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - HealthCity Chat".

3

Option C: Institutional Administrator

Contact your hospital or organization’s HealthCity Chat administrative lead, who possesses console authorization to decommission and purge user profiles.

Children’s Privacy

Exclusivity to licensed healthcare professionals and age verification standards

HealthCity Chat is strictly designed, marketed, and provisioned for licensed healthcare professionals, hospital administrators, healthcare directors, and clinical team members.

Our services are not directed at, marketed to, or intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 17 in applicable jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from minors.

If we discover or are notified by a parent, guardian, or healthcare institution that an individual under the age of 18 has inadvertently created an account or accessed the platform without lawful authorization, we will immediately deactivate the account, sever all active sessions, and expeditiously erase all associated personal information from our servers.
